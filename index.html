About The Artist

Where emotion meets the bansuri, rooted in Indian tradition and modern expression.

Tanmoy Pramanik (Tanmoy Flute) is an Indian bansuri performer, recording artist, and flute educator whose musical journey bridges classical depth, contemporary melody, and expressive instrumental storytelling.

His signature sound is defined by nuanced breath control, lyrical phrasing, and an emotive touch that transforms ragas, fusion compositions, and beloved melodies into deeply resonant musical experiences for live concerts and digital audiences.

Classical Foundations

Grounded in the discipline of Hindustani classical music, exploring raga development, swara precision, and meditative acoustic resonance.

Contemporary & Fusion Exploration

Blending bansuri textures with guitars, beatboxing, keyboard soundscapes, and cinematic arrangements to bring Indian flute to diverse audiences.

Performance & Flute Education

Dedicated to soulful stage presentations, polished studio recordings, and empowering the next generation of flute learners through structured training.

Musical Journey

A Journey Through Music

The artistic path and key milestones shaping Tanmoy Pramanik's musical evolution.

Milestone 01

Musical Beginnings

The discovery of the Indian bamboo flute (bansuri). Cultivating the initial embouchure, breath discipline, and a lifelong emotional connection to acoustic airflow.

Milestone 02

Classical Foundations

Deep immersion in Hindustani classical music, exploring raga structures, swara intonation, meend, and the spiritual depth of traditional flute gayaki.

Milestone 03

Exploring Contemporary & Fusion Music

Expanding sonic boundaries through cross-genre collaboration—combining bansuri with beatboxers, acoustic guitars, and keys in projects such as TRIMAD.

Milestone 04

Performances & Collaborations

Bringing melodic flute storytelling to live concert stages, studio recording sessions, romantic covers, and creative collaborative visual releases.

Milestone 05

Current Musical Projects & Digital Stage

Connecting with listeners worldwide through cinematic releases on YouTube and Instagram, building classical practice tools, and mentoring students in flute mastery.
